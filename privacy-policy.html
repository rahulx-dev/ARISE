ARISE PROTOCOL

LOCAL-FIRST DISCIPLINE ARCHITECTURE & CLOUD REWARDS

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Overview

ARISE ("we", "our", or "the App") is a physical fitness gamification and screen-time discipline application. We respect your privacy and are committed to transparent, accurate data handling. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, store, and process device permissions, telemetry, and transactional information.

2. Data Collection, Permissions & Purpose

  • Camera Access (Live Pose Detection): Used in real-time on-device memory via MediaPipe to verify exercise reps (pushups, squats). Live workout camera frames are processed in volatile memory and are not recorded, streamed, or uploaded to any remote server. This applies to pose-estimation frames only — physique progress photos (below) are a separate feature and are saved on-device.
  • Physique Progress Photos: If you choose to capture progress photos to track physical changes, these photos are persisted locally in your device's private app storage. They are never shared publicly or uploaded to cloud storage without your explicit action.
  • Installed App Inventory & App Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Used locally to identify installed launcher-capable apps so you can configure app limits, and to track active screen time on designated restricted apps. No private message contents, keystrokes, browsing history, or in-app activity are ever accessed.
  • Background Location (Run Tracking): When you start a run, the app uses background location (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION) to measure distance, pace, and route while the screen is off or the app is in the background. Run coordinates are stored on-device only and are never uploaded to our servers or any third party.
  • Display Over Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Used to render the discipline lock screen over restricted apps once daily screen-time quotas expire.
  • Battery Optimization Exemption & Foreground Service: Used to maintain continuous screen-time monitoring and prevent OEM operating systems from killing the monitor service.
  • Notifications: Used for daily streak alerts, workout reminders, and quest completion notices.
  • Account & Identity (Google Sign-In): When you sign in with Google, we collect your verified name and email address to authenticate your session and link your cloud progression save and leaderboard rank.
  • Payment & Financial Data (UPI ID): When you submit a cashout request for earned coins, your destination UPI address and requested amount are transmitted over TLS and stored in our Supabase backend database solely to disburse and fulfill your withdrawal. UPI IDs are not end-to-end or field-level encrypted at rest — they are protected by TLS in transit and Supabase's access controls at rest.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

The App integrates with the following third-party SDKs to provide essential features:

  • Supabase: Authenticated database and backend procedures for cloud progression saves, leaderboard ranking, and payout fulfillment.
  • Sentry: Application performance monitoring and crash diagnostics (device model, OS version, stack traces, and your account ID so we can correlate crashes). No sensitive user financial details or passwords are sent.
  • Google AdMob: Serves rewarded and banner advertisements. AdMob collects standard advertising IDs in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies.
  • Google Sign-In: Secure PKCE authentication for Google account linkage.
  • CPX Research / BitLabs: Optional third-party offerwalls for optional survey rewards. When you open a survey offerwall, your account identifier (ext_user_id / uid) is shared with that provider so they can credit your rewards. Their own privacy policies apply to data they collect inside their surveys.

4. Data Retention & User Controls

Local data (settings, exercise sessions, local progress photos) can be reset at any time via Settings → Storage / Clear App Data.

Cloud data associated with your Google account (cloud saves, leaderboard ranking) is retained while your account remains active.

5. Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated personal data. In the app, go to Settings → Storage & Data Deletion → Delete My Account: this immediately deletes all local data, your cloud save, and signs you out.

Your Supabase login record, wallet, and leaderboard rows cannot be deleted from the app (they require server-side access). To remove them, email us from your linked Google address:

Email: support@ariseapp.dev

Upon verification, all remaining associated records are permanently deleted within 30 days.
