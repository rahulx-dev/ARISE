100% PRIVATE ON-DEVICE ARCHITECTURE

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Overview

ARISE ("we", "our", or "the App") is an AI-powered physical fitness gamification and screen-time discipline application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal data. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle device permissions, telemetry, and transactional information.

2. Device Permissions & Usage

  • Camera Access (AI Pose Estimation): The camera feed is processed in real-time exclusively on your local device RAM using Google ML Kit. Camera frames, images, and video streams are NEVER recorded, saved to disk, or transmitted to any external server.
  • App Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Used locally to track active screen time across distraction apps that you designate as restricted. No private messages, keystrokes, browsing history, or in-app activity are ever accessed.
  • Display Over Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Used to render the discipline lock screen on top of restricted apps when time quotas expire.
  • Battery Optimization Exemption: Used to prevent Android operating system vendors from terminating background monitoring services unexpectedly.
  • Notifications: Used for daily streak reminders, quest completions, and workout alerts.

3. Cloud Payouts & Anti-Cheat Ledger

When you submit a UPI cashout request in the Shop Vault:

  • We collect your chosen UPI ID, requested withdrawal amount, and anonymous device identifier.
  • This data is encrypted in transit and stored in our secure Supabase PostgreSQL cloud database solely for the purpose of processing your monetary disbursement.
  • We do not sell, rent, or monetize your payment information with third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with the following trusted third-party providers:

  • Google AdMob: For displaying rewarded and interstitial advertisements. AdMob may collect device advertising IDs in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies.
  • Google ML Kit: On-device computer vision libraries for pose landmark extraction.
  • Supabase: Cloud database infrastructure for verifying coin redemptions and payout requests.
